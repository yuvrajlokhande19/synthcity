4. KEY FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS & BUSINESS LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STRICT UNDER 5-LINE AI RESPONSES:
   - Every AI response across the web dashboard and Telegram MUST be under 4-5 concise lines (max 60 words).
   - System automatically splits output by line breaks and truncates `"\n".join(ans_lines[:4])`.

2. NATURAL HUMANIZED WORKER DISPATCH PARSING:
   - When user prompts: *"send worker 1 message to clean metro station near sitabuldi..."*,
     *"send message to all worker to complete there task..."*, *"clean sitaburdi fort as priority send message to both of them"*, or *"send all workers to clean zone 3 heritage sites..."*:
     - Admin AI uses Gemini to synthesize a warm, friendly 1-liner for the worker (e.g. *"Hi U, please clean the Sitabuldi Metro station area and clear entrance debris."*).
     - The raw user meta-instruction is NEVER sent to the workers.
     - Dual-Worker Priority Guarantee: Keywords like "both of them", "to both", "both workers", "all workers" or priority alerts automatically queue outbox items for BOTH Worker 1 (U) and Worker 2 (Ritesh).
     - Outbox Fallback Guarantee: If Worker 2 ID is not yet assigned a numeric Telegram ID, the outbox listener safely routes the dispatch to active Telegram user ID (`WORKER_1_ID`) so the alert is received instantly on Telegram.
     - The message is pushed to `state["dashboard_outbox"]` and delivered via Telegram immediately.
     - The task is logged in `state["workers"][target]["pending_tasks"]`.

3. SMART HERITAGE SITE & AREA DIVISION:
   - Prompting to divide Zone 3 heritage sites by area splits landmarks:
     - Worker 1 (U): Ambazari Heritage Lake Promenade & Pavilion (1.2 sq km area).
     - Worker 2 (Ritesh): Futala Heritage Promenade & MIDC Enclave (0.9 sq km area).

4. 2-WAY TELEGRAM CITIZEN & WORKER LOOP:
   - Citizen reports issue on Telegram -> Bot dispatches alert to Worker 1 & Worker 2.
   - Worker replies on Telegram ("ok I will do this") -> Bot auto-notifies Citizen on Telegram ("Hi Dhynendra, your issue has been assigned to Worker U and is being handled now!").
   - Worker asks *"what are my tasks"* on Telegram -> Bot returns active pending tasks list from `c2_state.json`.

5. 30-MINUTE GOOGLE DOCS AUTO-SYNC:
   - Background thread `update_google_docs_news_loop` fetches Zone 1, 2, and 3 Google Docs every 30 minutes, extracts top 3 bullet points, and records sync timestamp for UI relative time badges ("Synced just now", "Synced 5m ago").
